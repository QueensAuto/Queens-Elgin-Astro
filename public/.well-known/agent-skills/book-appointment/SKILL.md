# Book an appointment at Queens Auto Services Elgin

Only book with details the customer has given you, and only after they confirm
the day, time and service. The shop confirms every request with the customer
by phone or email.

## Option 1: offer page booking tool (preferred)
These pages have a booking form, and in browsers with WebMCP they register a
tool named `request_appointment`:
- Brakes ($369 pads and rotors per axle): https://queensautoserviceselgin.com/brakes/
- Alignment ($35 off): https://queensautoserviceselgin.com/wheel-alignment/
- Any other repair (up to $45 off): https://queensautoserviceselgin.com/auto-repair-discount/

Pick the page that matches the job. The tool needs: first_name, email, phone
(10-digit US), vehicle_year, vehicle_make, vehicle_model, date (YYYY-MM-DD,
America/Chicago) and time; last_name and comments are optional.

Bookable slots (30 minutes):
- Monday-Friday: 08:00 AM-12:30 PM and 02:00 PM-03:30 PM
- Saturday: 08:00 AM-01:30 PM
- No bookings on Sundays, Thanksgiving, Dec 24, Dec 25, Dec 31 or Jan 1.

The reply includes the customer's savings code; pass it on to them. Without
WebMCP, fill in the same form on the page.

## Option 2: online scheduler
https://queensautoserviceselgin.com/request-an-appointment/ (Shopmonkey scheduler; needs JavaScript).

## Option 3: phone
Call (224) 635-3000 during business hours (Mon-Fri 7:30 AM-6:00 PM,
Sat 7:30 AM-4:00 PM).

## Do not
- Do not submit a booking the customer has not approved.
- Do not promise a price beyond the published offers; the shop quotes the rest.
